Clarified:
- The project has no current Production command category named general
  operation stop, monitoring-only stop, safe stop, graceful stop, drain request,
  participant-removal stop, or global STOP writer.
- `STOPPED` is a lifecycle or projection state, not a user command or command
  entry point.
- `RUNNING`, current-session participation, realtime registration, and live-test
  cleanup needs do not authorize creation of a new operation-stop service.
- Emergency Stop remains the forced interruption family.
- Close, early close, automatic close, liquidation, and completion policies
  remain the normal completion families.
