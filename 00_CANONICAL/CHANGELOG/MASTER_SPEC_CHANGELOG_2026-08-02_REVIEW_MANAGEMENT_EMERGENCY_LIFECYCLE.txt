Clarified:

- `상태`, `사유`, and `검출` are separate Review Management columns with separate
  meanings.
- `검출` is the actual detection point of the review cause, not the location
  from which the Review Management window was opened.
- Review Management status must not be derived from reason-string comparison.
- `display_status` is the Review Management status Source of Truth supplied by
  the collector.
- `return_availability` and `display_status` are different concepts; local
  compatibility overlap remains a follow-up.
