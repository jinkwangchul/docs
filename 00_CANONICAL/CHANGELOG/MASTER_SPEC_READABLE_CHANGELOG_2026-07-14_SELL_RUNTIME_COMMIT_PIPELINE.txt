BUY budget policy correction:
- The old fixed X2/X3 style increase wording is no longer the official policy.
- Initial setup is now described as "최초 예산 설정" with either "주수 설정" or "예산 설정".
- Increase methods are now "회차 기준" and "금액 기준".
- 회차 기준 supports direct operator input with + or ×.
- 금액 기준 supports direct operator input with × only.
- 예산 설정 means a maximum budget for the round; the actual order quantity is calculated later from the current order price and must stay within budget and other order limits.

Automatic order execution flow update:
- The production flow now records the completed path from Routine Signal through approval, Operation Policy Gate, Execution Enable, REAL_READY, ORDER_QUEUED, Final Send Gate, dispatch claim, and SendOrder callable result recording.
- SendOrder results are recorded as `SEND_CALL_ACCEPTED`, `SEND_CALL_REJECTED`, or `SEND_UNCERTAIN`.
- Live Chejan order/fill events now feed the Queue lifecycle, Fill ledger, and Position update path.
- Missing Fill or Position follow-up can be retried by Chejan event identity without duplicating completed work.
- Kiwoom gubun=1 holding events are stored in `runtime/broker_holdings.json`.
- Broker holdings are compared against internal positions for reconciliation, but they do not automatically modify `positions.json`.

- `runtime/positions.json`: internal Position based on Fill evidence.
- `runtime/broker_holdings.json`: live Kiwoom broker holding snapshot.
