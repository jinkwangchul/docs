MASTER_SPEC_READABLE_CHANGELOG_2026-07-15_AI_ARCHITECTURE_CONTAMINATION_POLICY

What changed:

The project documentation now records a stronger rule against AI-created structure contamination.

The new policy says that the largest structural risk is AI repeatedly splitting small implementation units into independent layers, then mistaking local modularity for whole-system correctness.

Main additions:

- New AI architecture contamination policy in AGENT_WORKFLOW_REFERENCE.
- New Canonical supplement for minimum structural unit rules.
- Stronger rule that integration must be considered before separation.
- Stronger approval conditions for new layers.
- Clear warning that unit-test success does not prove production call graph completeness.
- Forced structural audit triggers when new layers, writers, durable states, or Source of Truth boundaries are proposed.
- User approval remains required before deletion, integration, or structural change.

What did not change:

- No code files were changed.
- No runtime files were changed.
- No queue, rules, or stocks files were changed.
- Existing Canonical files were preserved.
- Existing dirty/untracked files were preserved.
