Signal expression contract
--------------------------

- Supported operators: AND, OR, binary NOT, and parentheses.
- Parentheses are evaluated first.
- Operators outside parentheses are evaluated from left to right.
- Binary NOT is identity-set difference: identities in the LEFT result that
  overlap the RIGHT result are removed. It is not boolean negation.
- Invalid, incomplete, or unsupported expressions fail closed.
- The same parsed AST travels through Preview, Approval, Commit, and the
  Production evaluator.

SELL A/B/C contract
-------------------

- Each of A, B, and C has three rows and two user-selected operators.
- All six operators are connected to Production execution.
- Each group evaluates as `(ROW1 OP1 ROW2) OP2 ROW3`.
- The user-selected AND, OR, and binary NOT values are preserved.
- A/B/C group identities remain distinct through mapping and evaluation.

SELL A OCR contract
-------------------

- The OCR source is the MACD Oscillator histogram.
- Threshold, comparison, and direction are user-controlled values.
- Transition offsets are: first transition bar = 0, next = 1, next = 2.
- No new consecutive-bar suppression or re-arm policy is implied.
- The operational outcome of the configured values remains the operator's
  settings responsibility.

SELL B Price Box contract
-------------------------

Kiwoom Hero 4 basic-indicator evidence supplied by the user:

  Price        = CLOSE
  MA period    = 24
  MA method    = SIMPLE
  A            = MA(CLOSE, 24, SIMPLE)
  Middle       = A
  Upper        = A + AvgIf(CLOSE - A, 1, 0.0)
                   + 2 * StdevIf(CLOSE - A, 1, 0.0)
  Lower        = A + AvgIf(CLOSE - A, -1, 0.0)
                   - 2 * StdevIf(CLOSE - A, -1, 0.0)

AvgIf/StdevIf operate on matching deviations from the full candle dataset
supplied to the indicator. Historical no-lookahead is an input-availability
boundary: a historical caller supplies candles only through its cutoff. The
indicator formula itself is not rewritten as a prefix-cumulative formula.

Direction mapping:

- upward -> PRICE_BOX_UPPER
- downward -> PRICE_BOX_LOWER

Comparison mapping:

- at least p percent -> selected line * (1 + p / 100), then >=
- at most p percent  -> selected line * (1 - p / 100), then <=

`p` is the user's value. Price Box can be the sole active condition.

Non-blocking parity note:

The official N versus N-1 divisor was not exposed by the acquired evidence.
The current project population-variance convention is retained.

BUY price-comparison contract
-----------------------------

Both mutually exclusive branches are retained:

- average price <= actionable order price
- average price > actionable order price

The actual price relationship selects exactly one configured branch. Each
branch independently preserves its mode, operator, and user values.

ROUND ADD:
(confirmed previous buy round + user value) * starting budget

ROUND MULTIPLY:
(confirmed previous buy round * user value) * starting budget

BUDGET:
previous budget * user ratio

No fixed example number is an execution semantic. Price-comparison ACTIVE_BUY
remains unsupported and fail closed; it is distinct from Last-Round Active Buy.

Duplicate-signal contract
-------------------------

A duplicate exists when a later signal arrives before the earlier signal flow
has completed.

LEADING priority:

- The leading flow continues.
- Overlapping later signals are durably neutralized/CANCELLED.

TRAILING priority:

- Outstanding predecessor orders are canceled or neutralized.
- Cancel effect and actual fills are reconciled.
- Latest Position/Broker holding consistency is required.
- The predecessor is durably neutralized before the successor progresses.

The behavior is symmetric for BUY-to-SELL and SELL-to-BUY overlap. Normal
duplicate arbitration cancellation is not an execution error.

Error-policy contract
---------------------

An error means an abnormal Routine flow such as an omitted BUY/SELL result or
an equivalent non-regular execution result.

STOP_AND_REVIEW:

- Records Production Event evidence.
- Creates Review Management classification/evidence.

CONTINUE_NEXT_CYCLE:

- Records Production Event evidence.
- Records cycle-failure evidence.
- Allows the next normal cycle to continue.
