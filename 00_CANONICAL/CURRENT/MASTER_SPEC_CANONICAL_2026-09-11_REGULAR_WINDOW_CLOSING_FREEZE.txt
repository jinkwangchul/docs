1. Authority Boundary

This Freeze records only the durable contracts implemented and verified after
the 2026-09-09 Freeze. Older independent Candle, Broker, Queue, Position, Fill,
Review, Close, Routine, and ATS owners remain authoritative for unaffected
scope. No Runtime, stock data, operation-policy setting, schema, migration, or
Broker execution change is authorized by this document.

2. Operation-Mode Admission

Production pre-start operation-mode admission uses current process/session
participation rather than stale persisted RUNNING alone. A real current
participant that is RUNNING remains blocked, and independent safety guards are
unchanged. A BLOCK/FAIL with no user choice uses Toast; important operational
failure also records Event evidence with the detailed reason.

Mock WAITING or VALIDATION_STOPPED with no active Operation may edit operation
mode, selected ATS sessions, and operation schedule without Broker login/account
authorization. Initial-buy budget and any fresh-price-dependent calculation
retain their existing authorization and freshness requirements. RUNNING,
CLOSING, ERROR, ENDED, and REVIEW_STOPPED guards remain fail-closed.

3. Close And Display

The detailed matrix is owned by
`MASTER_SPEC_CANONICAL_2026-09-11_OPERATION_MODE_CLOSE_LIQUIDATION_DISPLAY_RECONCILIATION.txt`.
An active Operation uses its immutable snapshot. Next effective settings do not
hot-swap it. CONTINUOUS manual/manual+ATS does not create AUTO close from session
FINAL_END; progression may stop while current status projects `감시/대기` and
liquidation projects `-`. SCHEDULED retains its configured policy and existing
time-triggered AUTO-close route. Legitimate Early Close and individual/immediate
liquidation remain preserved.

4. Monitoring And Quick Chart

Production and Mock Quick Chart headers refresh status, method, liquidation,
and the operation badge from the same current semantic Projection used by Main.
The 1,000 ms header refresh performs no candle/marker/PnL rebuild, Broker/TR
request, or Runtime write.

The shared chart renderer draws a display-only bridge from the last valid
confirmed plotted price point to the current-price marker. It does not append a
price to `close_series`, create a fake candle, rewrite history, or persist bridge
state. A new confirmed bar moves the bridge origin; past-date charts without a
live marker draw no bridge. Production and Mock use the same renderer.

5. TR Capacity Gauge

TR capacity is an internal processing-load observation based on recent 60-second
actual dispatch count divided by the existing centralized governor pacing
capacity. It is not an official Kiwoom remaining quota, maximum-stock count, or
stability guarantee. Queue depth and wait remain supplementary evidence, and
the indicator itself generates no TR.

The completed Main UI is six equal QFrame segments with identical geometry and
spacing. Inactive segments are gray. Active position colors are green, green,
orange, orange, red, red. Levels 0 through 6 mean respectively `비용 없음`,
`매우양호`, `양호`, `아주보통`, `보통`, `부담`, and `매우부담`. Unicode box/bar
characters and the former blue character indicator are absent. Actual GUI
acceptance verified 0/1/3/6 states and the existing Monitoring-window click.

6. Registration, Identity, And Unregister Integrity

Mock direct registration defers new base-stock mutation until at least one
Routine is selected in the shared Routine-selection dialog. Zero selection or
cancel creates neither base Stock nor Mock Session/Instance. Existing Stock
identity and Production assignment remain unchanged.

Mock valid-stock flat projection preserves Stock x RoutineInstance identity,
context-menu target, Quick Chart target, effective settings, and Session ID.
Routine unregister fails closed while canonical assigned Stocks or current Mock

7. Routine Settings And Context Interaction

validation-on-commit Supplemental. Sell-expression validation is based on the

8. Cycle And Performance Separation

After normal close and liquidation completion, current monitoring-cycle
aggregation resets at the completion boundary. An abnormal cycle preserves its
evidence until operator intervention restores integrity; only then may the
current cycle reset.

Monitoring-window metrics are current-cycle values. AutoTradeSetting
performance/history is completed-cycle history. Resetting the monitoring cycle
does not delete historical performance.
