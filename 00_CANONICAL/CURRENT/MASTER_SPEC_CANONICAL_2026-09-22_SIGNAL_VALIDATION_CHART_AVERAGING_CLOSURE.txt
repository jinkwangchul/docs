5. VALIDATION-ONLY AVERAGING / VIRTUAL EXECUTION

The compact Validation averaging controls are chart-local execution simulation
controls. They are not Production order authorization and do not directly mutate
Routine registration/change execution settings.

Mode names shown to the operator are:
- 회차기준 / ROUND
- 예산기준 / BUDGET
- 능동매수 / ACTIVE_BUY

Legacy labels 회차증가 and 금액증가 remain accepted only as compatibility inputs.

5.1 First buy

The Validation starting budget is expressed as a whole-share quantity. Validation
estimated BUY/SELL fills use the same completed-Candle CLOSE used by signal/filter
evaluation and update weighted average price, cumulative quantity, cycle cost, and
completed-cycle return.

5.2 회차기준

ROUND uses the fixed starting budget as its origin and derives each repeat budget
from the confirmed previous round number plus/multiplied by the configured value.
It does not compound the previous calculated budget.

5.3 예산기준

BUDGET ratio must be greater than 1.0.

The next round uses the previous approved calculated budget, not the previous
actual filled amount. Integer-share sizing is floor(calculated_budget/current
price). The difference between calculated budget and effective order spend is not
carried as a separate cash remainder and does not shrink the next budget basis.

Illustrative fixed-price contract at 10,000 with starting quantity 1 and x1.3:
- round 1 approved basis 10,000 -> 1 share;
- round 2 calculated budget 13,000 -> 1 share;
- round 3 calculated budget 16,900 -> 1 share;
- round 4 calculated budget 21,970 -> 2 shares.

The following round compounds from 21,970, not from the 20,000 actually spent.

5.4 능동매수

2026-09-23 amendment:

ACTIVE_BUY is a corrective signal-price/average-price policy. The fixed reference
is SIGNAL_PRICE, captured at the BUY signal point. AVG_PRICE is the confirmed
position weighted average and changes only through confirmed fills.

The allowed corrective grammar is:
- 상향(UP): 평단가 is above 신호가; only 이하(<=) is actionable.
- 하향(DOWN): 평단가 is below 신호가; only 이하(<=) is actionable.
- 상하(BOTH): either side may be corrected; only 이내(WITHIN) is actionable.
- 이상(>=) and 이탈(OUTSIDE) are not Active-Buy actions because a BUY must not
  manufacture or enlarge the configured gap.

The minimum whole-share quantity is calculated from the actionable acquisition
price, but the projected weighted average must remain between the pre-buy average
and SIGNAL_PRICE. A projected average that would cross SIGNAL_PRICE is no-order.

Production strategy price-comparison axes are CURRENT_PRICE, SIGNAL_PRICE, and
AVG_PRICE. CURRENT_PRICE is live and may trigger an immediate state transition as
the market moves. SIGNAL_PRICE is fixed for the source signal. AVG_PRICE changes
only as confirmed fills change the position. Legacy strategy UI label 주문가 is
read-compatible as SIGNAL_PRICE, but new strategy comparison rules are written as
SIGNAL_PRICE. Actual order/fill prices remain execution evidence, not a fourth
strategy comparison axis.
