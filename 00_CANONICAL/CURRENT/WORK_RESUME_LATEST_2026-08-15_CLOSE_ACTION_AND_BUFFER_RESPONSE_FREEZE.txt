3. Completed Buffer-Response Execution Loop

- Official system-budget consumption projection is based on OPEN position cost
  plus confirmable unresolved BUY reservation.
- Stable entry increase creates a durable event and ownership record.
- One event owns at most one target; replay is idempotent across restart and
  Recovery.
- Candidate selection is fail-closed, returns zero or one stock, and supports
  the three confirmed single factors and two directions.
- `투입금액` ranking is `open_cost`; BUY reservation is not part of that
  ranking value.
- Below threshold: owned target uses `EARLY_CLOSE + routine`.
- At or above threshold: one existing buffer-owned EARLY_CLOSE pending target
  is escalated through BUY-only cancellation, confirmation, actual holding
  reconciliation, and `EARLY_CLOSE + market`.
- SELL cancellation, ordinary-holding expansion, predicted recovery, and
  multi-target escalation are prohibited.
- `evaluate_operation_close_completion()` provides completion evidence.
- Only `DONE` or `CARRYOVER_DONE` completes ownership.
- After actual completion the official budget projection is recalculated. If
  still at or above threshold, only one next owned pending target is escalated.
- Below-threshold, zero-buffer, and no-owned-target results stop or return
  non-execution without fabricating work.
