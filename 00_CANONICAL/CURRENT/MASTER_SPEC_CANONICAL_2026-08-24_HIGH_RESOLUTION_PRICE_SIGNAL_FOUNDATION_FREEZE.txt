==================================================
1. Authority And Purpose
==================================================

This Supplemental Canonical freezes the Production foundation implemented and
verified in High Resolution Price Signal Phases 2 through 8. Its purpose is to
receive normal automatic-trading market information at realtime Tick
resolution without adding TR polling, while keeping market-data reception
resolution separate from the Routine canonical-bar time axis.

This Freeze covers the Signal Foundation only. It does not declare a new
Routine signal semantic, indicator calculation, progress-bar semantic,
canonical candle definition, or order path.

It supplements and does not replace the 2026-08-20 Broker / Market Data
Architecture Final Freeze. Existing Current Canonicals remain authoritative
for canonical candle ownership, REALTIME_PRIMARY, reconciliation, Routine
execution, and order safety except for the narrower observation/UI contracts
recorded here.

Evidence classifications:

- IMPLEMENTED_AND_CODE_VERIFIED: present in the frozen Production baseline and
  covered by focused tests or static contract checks.
- SIMULATION_VERIFIED: verified by deterministic fake/mock or 32-bit Qt
  simulation; not a live-market guarantee.
- UNVERIFIED_EXTERNAL: not asserted as PASS or defect without live external
  evidence.

==================================================
5. High-Resolution Market State
==================================================

[IMPLEMENTED_AND_CODE_VERIFIED]

MarketDataHost owns the High-Resolution Market State process-locally.

- raw Tick queue: bounded FIFO, maximum depth 512
- Tick coalescing: none
- broker session validation: connection_epoch + login_session_id
- per-stock receive_sequence regression/replay rejection
- explicit queue overflow count and affected-stock uncertainty evidence
- data_quality: NORMAL or UNCERTAIN
- persistent storage: none

The newest event is rejected when the FIFO is full so the already queued FIFO
order remains intact. Overflow is visible evidence and changes affected state
quality to UNCERTAIN; it is not silently treated as complete market data.

A Tick from a stale session cannot overwrite the state established by the
current broker session.

==================================================
6. Price Signal Observation Gate
==================================================

[IMPLEMENTED_AND_CODE_VERIFIED]

The gate is process-local and defaults to OFF.

OFF preserves:

- realtime registration
- raw Tick reception
- High-Resolution Market State updates
- Realtime Shadow
- REALTIME_PRIMARY
- reconciliation
- canonical candle processing

OFF blocks only:

- the `high_resolution_price_observed` observation boundary
- live current-price chart projection

ON enables High-Resolution observation for valid current-session Tick state.
It does not connect the Tick to Routine signals, Queue, SendOrder, Chejan, or
the Execution Pipeline.

Changing the gate does not call SetRealReg, SetRealRemove, or CommRqData.
