MASTER SPEC CANONICAL
2026-09-11 Operation Mode x Close/Liquidation Display Contract Reconciliation

Status: CURRENT SUPPLEMENTAL AUTHORITY
Scope: Production and Mock operation-mode, close-admission, and display semantics

==================================================
1. Purpose And Authority
==================================================

This Supplemental Canonical resolves the conflict between the current user-
confirmed CONTINUOUS liquidation display contract and the 2026-08-26 Current
Canonical wording that could allow a configured liquidation value to appear
during ordinary CONTINUOUS operation.

For the display meanings defined here, authority order is:

1. this 2026-09-11 Supplemental Canonical;
2. the unaffected provisions of the 2026-08-26 Budget / Operation Start / ATS
   Freeze; and
3. the existing Close, Individual Liquidation, Mock, and GUI supporting owners.

Only the conflicting CONTINUOUS current-display meaning is superseded. The
2026-08-26 document remains historical evidence and remains current for its
unaffected Budget, Operation Start, participant, session, gap, and SCHEDULED
contracts.

==================================================
2. Supersession Boundary
==================================================

The following older interpretation is SUPERSEDED and MUST NOT be used for
current implementation:

- during ordinary CONTINUOUS / manual or manual+ATS operation with no operator
  Early Close and no legitimate liquidation entry, a stored or configured
  liquidation policy may cause the current liquidation cell to show or activate
  that configured value.

The following 2026-08-26 meanings remain current:

- status, method activity, liquidation text, liquidation activity, participant
  membership, and order permission are separate projections;
- ATS is a selected additional trading session rather than an independent
  operation mode;
- an ATS session end is not Operation end, automatic close, liquidation, or
  Review entry;
- SCHEDULED retains its configured time boundary and automatic-close route; and
- non-trading gaps block order progression without inventing a close route.

==================================================
3. Text And Active Are Independent
==================================================

`liquidation_text` and `liquidation_active` are different facts.

- `liquidation_active == false` does not by itself require text `-`.
- SCHEDULED may show a configured policy while the policy is inactive.
- CONTINUOUS ordinary operation shows `-` because no current close/liquidation
  route exists, not merely because the cell is inactive.
- A configured or historically preserved policy is not current execution
  evidence.

The display projection must not derive policy existence solely from whether the
final text is `-`, and it must not turn a configured SCHEDULED policy into `-`
merely because the policy is currently inactive.

==================================================
4. CONTINUOUS / Manual
==================================================

Unless the operator has entered Early Close or another legitimate liquidation
route, CONTINUOUS / manual has:

- automatic close: absent;
- regular close plus liquidation route: absent;
- liquidation text: `-`; and
- liquidation active: false.

This result is independent of time phase:

- PRE: `-`;
- ACTIVE: `-`;
- NON_TRADING_GAP: `-`; and
- FINAL_END: `-`.

FINAL_END in CONTINUOUS may stop time-permitted BUY/SELL progression. It does not
create close_source, close_method, automatic close, or liquidation evidence.

==================================================
5. CONTINUOUS / Manual+ATS
==================================================

CONTINUOUS / manual+ATS follows the same ordinary-operation liquidation rule:

- active selected ATS session: `-`;
- ATS gap: `-`;
- final selected ATS session end: `-`; and
- liquidation active: false.

ATS extends eligible trading time. It does not override the Routine-generated
BUY/SELL method and does not create automatic close, Early Close, liquidation,
or Operation-end evidence.

An operator Early Close or legitimate individual/immediate liquidation leaves
this ordinary-operation branch and follows the existing Close/Liquidation
owners.

==================================================
6. SCHEDULED / Timed Operation
==================================================

SCHEDULED retains the existing time-based close/liquidation contract.

If the configured policy is `5분/시장가`, the minimum display matrix is:

PRE_OPERATION_BOUNDARY:

- status: `감시/대기`;
- method: `루틴`, inactive;
- liquidation text: `5분/시장가`;
- liquidation active: false; and
- automatic close trigger: not yet due.

OPERATION_BOUNDARY_REACHED:

- status: `감시/대기` until the stock trading window is active;
- method: `루틴`, active under the existing phase contract;
- liquidation text: configured policy text;
- liquidation active: governed by the existing phase contract.

ACTIVE_TRADING_WINDOW:

- status: `매수/매도`;
- method: `루틴`, active;
- liquidation text: configured policy text;
- liquidation active: governed by the existing phase contract; and
- final order permission remains subject to every existing safety gate.

FINAL_END:

- configured policy text is not erased merely because the cell is inactive;
- the existing SCHEDULED automatic-close trigger remains permitted; and
- the resulting AUTO Close and liquidation phases follow the existing Close and
  Liquidation contracts.

==================================================
7. Legitimate Close And Liquidation
==================================================

The following are not ordinary CONTINUOUS display states and remain valid:

- operator Early Close;
- SCHEDULED AUTO Close;
- individual liquidation;
- immediate liquidation;
- MARKET liquidation;
- CURRENT_PRICE liquidation; and
- CARRYOVER where already authorized by its current owner.

During a legitimate route, the status, method, liquidation text, and active state
must come from the current close/liquidation evidence and its existing policy.
CARRYOVER does not become active MARKET/CURRENT_PRICE liquidation.

CURRENT_PRICE may leave an intentional residual holding. That does not authorize
generic ATS order-method restoration, forced MARKET conversion, or a new Review
bypass.

==================================================
8. WAITING And VALIDATION_STOPPED
==================================================

When no active Operation exists, WAITING and VALIDATION_STOPPED display the next
effective settings. A past stopped Operation remains historical evidence and
does not become current close/liquidation evidence.

- next effective CONTINUOUS: liquidation text `-`, inactive;
- next effective SCHEDULED: configured liquidation text, inactive.

When an active RUNNING or CLOSING Operation exists, its immutable Operation
snapshot remains authoritative. Mutable next-start settings must not hot-swap the
current Operation.

==================================================
9. Production / Mock / Main / Quick Chart Parity
==================================================

Production and Mock must use the same semantic result for the same operation
mode, time phase, Operation state, close evidence, ATS state, and liquidation
policy state.

Main and Quick Chart may use different UI adapters, but they must not disagree on:

- status text;
- method text and active state;
- liquidation text;
- liquidation policy existence; or
- liquidation active state.

No Main-only or Quick-Chart-only exception may hide an upstream projection
defect.
