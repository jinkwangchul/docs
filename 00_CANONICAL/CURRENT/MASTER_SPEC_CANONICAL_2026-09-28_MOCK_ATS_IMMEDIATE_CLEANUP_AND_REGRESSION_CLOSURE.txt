======================================================================

- when close_source is IMMEDIATE;
- when the currently evaluated time is inside an active selected ATS session;
- and when the regular-market pending-order cleanup boundary has already passed;

IMMEDIATE liquidation lifecycle.

The Host must therefore pass pending_order_cleanup_boundary=False to the

for active CONTINUOUS ATS IMMEDIATE liquidation.

The exception applies only while the ATS session is actually active. It does
not remove the common regular-end pending-order cleanup for ordinary close,
liquidation, carryover, expired ATS sessions, or unrelated Operations.

3. CURRENT_PRICE IMMEDIATE LIQUIDATION
======================================================================

CURRENT_PRICE liquidation remains a limit-price liquidation contract, not a
market-order guarantee.

During active ATS IMMEDIATE CURRENT_PRICE liquidation:
- fresh Mock trade evidence supplies the current-price limit;
- a Mock SELL LIMIT order may be created;
- normal virtual orderbook fill rules decide fills and residual quantity.

The cleanup fix does not convert CURRENT_PRICE to MARKET and does not guarantee
that every CURRENT_PRICE liquidation ends with zero holding in arbitrary market
evidence.

The focused regression uses executable evidence where bid and current price
permit the SELL LIMIT to fill, proving that the previous failure was the
regular-cleanup boundary suppressing order creation rather than a price-match
rule.
