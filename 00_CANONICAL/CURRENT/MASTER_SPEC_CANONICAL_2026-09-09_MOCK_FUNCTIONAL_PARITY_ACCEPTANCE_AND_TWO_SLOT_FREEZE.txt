8. Indicator Follow Situation Price Response Two-Slot Contract

SETTING1 and SETTING2 are independent price-response filters. Each owns its own
enabled, source/left, target/right, direction, threshold/ratio, comparator, and
action values:

SETTING1: IF condition_1 THEN action_1
SETTING2: IF condition_2 THEN action_2

The slot number has no fixed upper/lower, rise/fall, or priority meaning. All of
the following are valid when each individual slot is valid:

- UP + DOWN, UP + UP, and DOWN + DOWN;
- same or different source/target;
- same or different thresholds and comparators;
- same or different actions; and
- overlapping or disjoint price regions.

The former mapper/commit-validator cross-slot rejection for overlapping regions
with different actions was not authorized and is removed. Single-slot behavior
and per-slot invalid-input fail-closed validation remain. The higher-level
`미체결` versus `가격비교` mutual-exclusion contract remains unchanged.

The Actual GUI round trip was verified with:

- SETTING1: AVG_PRICE -> CURRENT_PRICE, UP, 0.15, >=, CANCEL_BATCH;
- SETTING2: AVG_PRICE -> CURRENT_PRICE, DOWN, 0.10, <=, RESET.

Save and reopen preserved both slots without value loss, exchange, or collapse.
