2. Normalized Close Action Contract

Operator-facing close actions are projections of the existing common
EARLY_CLOSE Production contract:

- `조기마감` = `EARLY_CLOSE` with liquidation method `routine`.
- `즉시청산` = `EARLY_CLOSE` with liquidation method `market`.

These labels do not define two independent close systems. State, method, and
liquidation remain separate display concepts, while execution continues through
the existing close, order, completion, Recovery, and reconciliation pipelines.

The normalized projection applies to:

- stock-instance chart controls;
- Main Monitoring routine-category actions;
- Main Monitoring routine-instance actions; and
- buffer-response policy and execution.

The automatic-trading settings global early-close method remains
`operation_policy.json > early_close.method`.

Entry-point precedence is fixed as follows:

- In a paired `조기마감 / 즉시청산` control, the two labels explicitly select
  `routine` and `market`, respectively.
- A standalone `조기마감` control uses the current
  `operation_policy.json > early_close.method`; the paired-control rule must not
  hard-code that standalone action to `routine`.
- A stock context-menu command uses the one-shot method selected by the operator
  for that invocation: `루틴마감`, `시장가`, `현재가`, `손/익절`, or `이월`.
  The environment default may initialize the choice but must not overwrite the
  explicit context-menu selection, and the one-shot selection must not rewrite
  the environment default.

All three entry forms retain the same canonical state, method, liquidation,
Recovery, Queue, order, fill, and completion contracts. A stock-level one-shot
method does not create a separate durable operation mode.

3. Removed Excess Immediate-Liquidation Contract

The following separate contract surface is removed from Production Python:

- `immediate_liquidation_request`;
- `IMMEDIATE_LIQUIDATION_REQUEST_KEY`;
- `IMMEDIATE_LIQUIDATION_STATUS_REQUESTED`;

`IMMEDIATE_LIQUIDATION_REQUIRED` may remain only as a durable buffer-response
policy/ownership intent. It is not an OperationCommand, an executor, or an
independent stock operation state. Its execution meaning is the normalized
`EARLY_CLOSE + market` route.

4. System-Budget Buffer Basis

- Buffer response uses the confirmed system-level total budget contract.
- The canonical storage is `operation_policy.json > system_budget` with
  `total_budget` and `available_budget_percent`.
- `available_budget_percent` is the only stored available/buffer ratio value.
  `buffer_budget_percent` is derived as `100 - available_budget_percent` and is
  not an independent Source of Truth.
- The system-level amounts are projected as
  `available_budget = floor(total_budget * available_budget_percent / 100)` and
  `buffer_budget = total_budget - available_budget`; any integer remainder stays
  in the buffer so the two amounts always equal `total_budget`.
- `available_budget_percent == 100` means buffer disabled. In that state the
  stored value remains 100 while the UI may project inactive ratio/entry fields
  as `-`.
- This system-level ratio and amount contract refines only the 2026-08-14
  Canonical statements that the available/buffer calculation and storage were
  then unconfirmed. The 2026-08-14 prohibition on routine, instance, and stock
  allocation remains fully in force.
- Buffer is a response region inside the one system total-budget model. It is
  not a routine budget, stock budget, reserved portfolio allocation, or a new
  hierarchical allocation system.
- Buffer consumption is projected from actual OPEN position cost basis plus
  unresolved BUY reservation that can be confirmed safely.
- SELL orders do not increase budget consumption.
- Filled BUY quantity belongs to position cost; only remaining BUY quantity may
  belong to open reservation, preventing double counting.
- Missing, malformed, account-mismatched, or otherwise uncertain evidence is
  fail-closed.
- Routine, routine-instance, or stock budget allocation is not introduced.

5. Stable Entry, Event, And Ownership

- A stable buffer-entry increase creates a durable event and ownership record.
- A single event can own at most one selected stock target.
- Event identity, target ownership, response intent, and dispatch/completion
  evidence survive process restart and Recovery.
- Reprocessing the same event is idempotent and must not select a new stock.
- A new stable increase may create a new event and select the next eligible
  stock independently.
- The event unit is one stable buffer-entry increase batch. Distinct stable
  batches may accumulate EARLY_CLOSE ownership on multiple stocks over time,
  while each individual event still owns at most one target.
- Crossing the configured threshold does not convert every accumulated
  EARLY_CLOSE ownership to market close at once.
- Ordinary holdings are never converted into buffer-owned targets merely
  because the current entry ratio is high.

6. Candidate And Policy Contract

- Candidate selection uses only actually OPEN, auto-trade-owned positions with
  confirmable snapshots.
- Stocks already in close, liquidation, SELL progression, or another confirmed
  termination flow are excluded fail-closed.
- One policy evaluation returns zero or one new target.
- Supported single evaluation factors are cumulative profit amount,
  cumulative profit rate, and OPEN position cost.
- `투입금액` ranking uses `open_cost`; open BUY reservation is not mixed into
  that ranking value.
- Direction is only high-to-low or low-to-high.
- Exact ties use stable stock identity only; no secondary strategy score is
  introduced.

6.1 Buffer-Response Settings Surface

- `일괄적용` and `손익별 적용` are mutually exclusive application modes.
- `손익별 적용` contains exactly `▪ 수익` and `▪ 손실`. The selected row is
  based on the confirmable total automatic-trading cumulative PnL amount:
  total amount greater than or equal to zero selects `수익`; a negative total
  selects `손실`.
- Each active strategy row has exactly one evaluation factor and one direction.
  The retired first/second/third-priority model must not be recreated.
- Evaluation factors are exactly `손익비율`, `손익금액`, and `투입금액`.
  Directions are exactly `높은순` and `낮은순`. No secondary score or implicit
  tie-breaking strategy is authorized.
- Each row owns an independent response badge. Only a double-click cycles
  `조기마감 -> 즉시청산 -> 구간마감 -> 조기마감`; a single click changes
  nothing, and a disabled row cannot cycle.
- The current Production policy reads the open settings surface fail-closed.
  Missing, closed, or invalid settings do not authorize an inferred default or
  an executable response.

6.2 Candidate Interpretation Boundary

- The stock whose activity happened to cross the threshold is not automatically
  the market-close target.
- A new stable event uses the configured single filter to select one eligible
  OPEN candidate. Threshold escalation re-evaluates only the existing
  buffer-owned EARLY_CLOSE pending pool with that filter.
- `손익금액` uses cumulative PnL amount, `손익비율` uses cumulative PnL rate,
  and `투입금액` uses OPEN position `open_cost`.

7. Threshold And Response Contract

The configured threshold `T` comes from the buffer-response setting and is not
hard-coded to 80.

- The UI allows 10 through 90 percent in 10-percent steps.
- `80%` is only the current initial display choice; it is not a Production
  constant.
- `T` is a buffer-entry-ratio threshold, not a time threshold.
- The display meaning is `조기마감  ◁ T ▷  즉시청산`.
- The `< T` and `>= T` split is applied when the row response mode is
  `구간마감`. Explicit `조기마감` or `즉시청산` badge choices retain their
  explicit response meaning.

- In `구간마감` mode below `T`, an owned target receives the normalized
  `EARLY_CLOSE + routine` action.
- In `구간마감` mode at or above `T`, only an existing buffer-owned EARLY_CLOSE
  pending target may be escalated.
- Escalation handles one stock at a time.
- The escalated target first uses BUY-only cancellation.
- SELL cancellation is prohibited.
- Cancellation confirmation and actual holding reconciliation must complete
  before the market close route proceeds.
- The execution route is the existing normalized `EARLY_CLOSE + market`
  pipeline; no new immediate executor exists.

8. Completion And Sequential Escalation

- Dispatch is not completion.
- Completion is accepted only from the existing
  `evaluate_operation_close_completion()` result.
- Only actual `DONE` or `CARRYOVER_DONE` evidence completes ownership.
- Predicted recovery or projected proceeds are not completion evidence.
- After completion, the official budget projection is evaluated again.
- If the buffer remains at or above `T`, at most one next buffer-owned
  EARLY_CLOSE pending target is escalated.
- The next target is never escalated before the previous target has actual
  completion evidence.
- If the buffer falls below `T`, escalation stops.
- Remaining buffer-owned targets stay in their EARLY_CLOSE state when
  escalation stops; falling below `T` does not clear or rewrite their ownership.
- If the buffer becomes zero, the response sequence terminates.
- If no eligible owned pending target exists, the result is non-execution.

9. Safety And Idempotency

- Recovery resumes durable ownership and intent without duplicating target
  selection, cancellation, close dispatch, or completion.
- One cycle cannot issue multiple escalations for the same ownership event.
- A buffer event cannot overwrite or duplicate an active buffer ownership held
  by another event. Conversely, buffer response cannot seize a stock that has
  another confirmed close, liquidation, or SELL flow. Any ownership or command
  conflict is fail-closed.
- BUY-only cancellation is scoped to unresolved BUY remainder for the selected
  target stock. A partial fill remains actual holding; only its unresolved BUY
  remainder may be cancelled.
- Uncertain cancellation confirmation blocks market close until confirmed
  state and actual holding reconciliation are available.
- Other stocks' BUY orders are not cancelled.
- SELL mutation is not introduced by buffer response.
- No separate immediate command, mode, executor, or fallback liquidation path
  may be reintroduced.
- Runtime and stock state are changed only by their existing canonical writers
  during real Production execution; verification uses mocks or isolated paths.
