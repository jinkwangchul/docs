==================================================

For the scope stated above, authority order is:

2. unaffected provisions of the 2026-09-12 Mock Close Lifecycle Parity

5. unaffected provisions of the 2026-08-15 Close Action And Buffer Response
   Freeze and the 2026-08-01 Close Process Operation Contract.

The following earlier meanings are superseded or clarified only within this
scope:

- `PROFIT_LOSS` is a close method, not a post-close liquidation method.  The
  2026-08-01 section 6 list that could be read as including `손/익절` among
  liquidation-policy execution methods is superseded by sections 5 and 10 of
  this document.
- Liquidation-stage `CARRYOVER` performs no action at `liquidation_start`:
  pending-order cancellation, SELL creation, and liquidation action are all
  zero at that boundary.  Its remaining pending orders are handled only by the
  common regular-end cleanup boundary defined in section 7, after which actual
  holding is reconciled and retained.  This rule supersedes any wording in this
  document that could require immediate cancellation at `liquidation_start`.
- Close-stage CARRYOVER and liquidation-stage CARRYOVER remain continuation
  routes, but their decision provenance is distinct and must not be collapsed.

No other historical or Current provision is globally retired.

==================================================
2. Three Independent Concepts
==================================================

A. Close policy

AUTO or EARLY cause enters a close stage.  `close_source` identifies why the
stage began.  `close_method` identifies how the close stage proceeds.  Source
and method are independent evidence.

B. Order execution

Cancellation, cancellation confirmation, fill confirmation, candidate, Queue,
SendOrder, Chejan, partial fill, order remainder, and Position reconciliation
belong to the existing execution layer.  Close and liquidation policies call
that layer; they do not create policy-specific partial-fill or re-quote
engines.

C. Liquidation policy

Liquidation is a stage after or overriding an unfinished close policy at the
applicable liquidation start boundary.  It is not merely the continuation of a
close order.  Close and liquidation must preserve distinct ownership and
decision provenance.

==================================================
3. Time Source And Absolute Boundary
==================================================

The global liquidation start time is derived only from the current Operation
snapshot of the existing operation policy:

  operation_policy.regular_market.end_time
  - liquidation.minutes_before_regular_close

Example:

  regular market 09:00-15:20
  liquidation five minutes before close
  -> liquidation start 15:15

The applicable per-stock one-Operation override, when present, replaces the
global minutes/method for that stock only.  Other stocks retain the global
snapshot.

The applicable liquidation start time is an absolute boundary that outranks an
unfinished close policy.  Before the boundary, operation or close policy may
progress.  At the boundary, the current close policy stops or hands off to the
liquidation stage.  A close policy must not keep its own order lifecycle active
past the liquidation start boundary.

The regular-market end time is the liquidation end boundary.  Liquidation
orders must not remain live beyond it.

Mutable settings for a future Operation must not hot-swap the active
Operation's snapshotted global policy or per-stock override.

==================================================
4. Liquidation Entry Sequence
==================================================

For MARKET and CURRENT_PRICE, the sequence at the applicable liquidation start
time is fixed:

1. stop Routine evaluation;
2. request cancellation of every non-terminal order owned by the Operation,
   including Routine and close-policy orders;
3. confirm cancellation and any fill received during cancellation;
4. establish final order states;
5. reconcile actual holding quantity; and
6. execute the frozen liquidation policy against that actual residual holding.

No liquidation SELL may be created before cancellation confirmation and
holding reconciliation.  This prevents a late SELL fill from producing an
over-sell or quantity mismatch.

If holding is zero after the same cancellation and reconciliation sequence, no
new liquidation order is created and normal completion may proceed.

Liquidation-stage CARRYOVER is the method-specific exception to this entry
sequence.  At `liquidation_start` it performs no cancellation, creates no SELL,
and starts no liquidation action.  Existing orders and holding remain unchanged
until the common regular-end pending-order cleanup boundary in section 7.

==================================================
5. Liquidation Methods
==================================================

The post-close liquidation methods are exactly:

- MARKET;
- CURRENT_PRICE; and
- CARRYOVER.

MARKET uses the existing market-order execution pipeline after the entry
sequence.  CURRENT_PRICE uses the existing canonical CURRENT_PRICE execution
boundary after the same sequence.  CARRYOVER performs no action at
`liquidation_start`.  At the common regular-end cleanup boundary, remaining
pending orders are cancelled, cancellation and intervening fills are confirmed,
actual holding is reconciled, no new SELL is created, and the holding is
retained.

`PROFIT_LOSS` is not a liquidation method.  It is the close method defined in
section 10.

==================================================
6. Liquidation Setting Lock
==================================================

Before the applicable liquidation start boundary, the global or per-stock
liquidation setting may be changed through its existing authorized writer.

At boundary entry, the final applicable setting is frozen for the current
Operation.  After entry, every policy replacement is blocked, including all
MARKET, CURRENT_PRICE, and CARRYOVER permutations.

The user-facing Toast text is exactly:

  청산설정변경이 불가능합니다.

Detailed evidence remains internal and must use the existing Event contract
where it can safely express at least timestamp, stock/instance/Operation
identity, liquidation start time, current policy, requested policy, block
reason, source, and relevant state.  This contract does not by itself authorize
a new Event schema.

==================================================
7. Regular-End Pending Cleanup And Termination Classification
==================================================

The common pending-order cleanup boundary is:

  pending_order_cancel_boundary
  = active Operation regular_market.end_time
    - pending_order_cancel_lead_seconds

The current default lead is 30 seconds.  For an active Operation ending at
15:20:00, cleanup therefore starts at 15:19:30.  This boundary applies to
MARKET, CURRENT_PRICE, and CARRYOVER.  At that boundary:

1. cancel every remaining pending Operation/liquidation order;
2. confirm cancellation and intervening fills;
3. reconcile actual holding; and
4. classify by termination route and provenance.

No method recreates a new order after this cleanup begins.  In particular,
liquidation-stage CARRYOVER reaches this point after performing no action at
`liquidation_start`; it then completes the common pending cleanup and retains
the reconciled holding with no new SELL.

Holding zero completes normally.

Positive holding is not classified from quantity alone.  The system must first
determine why the holding remains.

Normal holding-continuation routes, subject to the existing global
`review_policy.long_term_holding_enabled`, are:

- normal CONTINUOUS/manual or manual+ATS termination with no close or active
  liquidation execution;
- close-stage CARRYOVER; and
- liquidation-stage CARRYOVER after the common regular-end pending cleanup and
  holding-reconciliation sequence.

The following remain Review regardless of the long-term switch:

- residual holding after active MARKET liquidation;
- residual holding after active CURRENT_PRICE liquidation;
- any active-liquidation failure;
- unresolved pending order or cancellation;
- Recovery or reconciliation failure;
- Queue conflict;
- uncertain or rejected SendOrder;
- Broker/Runtime, identity, state, or configuration mismatch;
- forced termination; and
- any other integrity or safety failure.

Failed liquidation residual and explicit CARRYOVER are never equivalent.
Close-stage and liquidation-stage CARRYOVER preserve distinct provenance even
when both ultimately retain holding.

==================================================
8. ROUTINE Close
==================================================

ROUTINE close is a soft landing.  After close entry:

- Routine BUY and SELL evaluation continues;
- new BUY remains permitted by the existing permission contract;
- existing pending Routine orders are not cancelled merely because close
  began;
- the normal Routine path continues; and
- the final valid SELL boundary and existing completion evidence govern early
  completion.

Holding zero with no pending or recoverable execution may complete under the
existing zero-position contract.  At the applicable liquidation start boundary,
unfinished ROUTINE close yields to section 4.

==================================================
9. Hard-Landing Close Methods
==================================================

MARKET, CURRENT_PRICE, and PROFIT_LOSS are hard-landing close methods.  On
entry they suppress Routine BUY/SELL, cancel relevant pending orders, confirm
cancellation and intervening fills, and reconcile actual residual holding.

MARKET and CURRENT_PRICE may execute orders during the close stage through the
existing execution layer.  This is valid and is not itself close/liquidation
mixing.  Mixing exists only if close-stage and liquidation-stage ownership,
policy, evidence, or lifecycle remain indistinguishable at or after the
liquidation boundary.

Already filled quantity is never reversed when a close method changes.

==================================================
10. PROFIT_LOSS Close
==================================================

PROFIT_LOSS evaluates only the current open Position return:

  average acquisition price versus realtime current price

Realized PnL and cumulative Operation PnL are excluded.

Profit threshold only, loss threshold only, or both are valid.  Both absent is
invalid and cannot be saved or confirmed.

After entry, Routine signals are suppressed and pending orders pass through the
existing cancellation, confirmation, and holding-reconciliation boundary.
Before a threshold is reached, no new SELL is created.  At a threshold, the
then-current actual residual holding becomes the full SELL target, using
CURRENT_PRICE only.  Partial fill and remainder handling remain owned by the
existing execution layer.

Selecting MARKET is not an option inside PROFIT_LOSS.  It is a close-method
transition from PROFIT_LOSS to MARKET.

An unfinished PROFIT_LOSS close yields to the liquidation stage at the
applicable liquidation start boundary.

==================================================
11. Close-Stage CARRYOVER
==================================================

Close-stage CARRYOVER performs:

  all-order cancellation
  -> cancellation/fill confirmation
  -> actual holding reconciliation
  -> Routine signal suppression
  -> no new order
  -> HOLD
  -> close completion

It is explicit continuation intent and does not automatically enter
post-close liquidation.  A later explicit per-stock liquidation setting is
newer operator intent and may outrank the earlier close-stage CARRYOVER under
the existing intent-precedence contract.

==================================================
12. Close-Method Transition
==================================================

During the close stage the following transitions are allowed:

- ROUTINE to MARKET, CURRENT_PRICE, or PROFIT_LOSS;
- MARKET, CURRENT_PRICE, and PROFIT_LOSS among one another; and
- any close method to CARRYOVER.

For hard-landing transitions, the current close orders are cancelled, their
final cancellation/fill state is confirmed, actual residual holding is
reconciled, and only then is the new method applied.  A hard landing that has
entered execution cannot return to ROUTINE.

Changing method never changes `close_source`.

==================================================
13. AUTO And EARLY Source / Menu Contract
==================================================

AUTO and EARLY are causes, not methods.

- AUTO method changes preserve source AUTO.
- EARLY method changes preserve source EARLY.
- a one-Operation method override never mutates the environment default.

The existing close action/menu slot is reused; no parallel QAction is added.

- before EARLY: `조기마감`;
- after EARLY entry: `마감변경`;
- after AUTO entry: `마감변경`.

On first entry, the method is read from the active Operation snapshot's
corresponding default.  Reopening shows the actually applied current method.

==================================================
14. EARLY Return To Automatic Timeline
==================================================

Within the EARLY `마감변경` choices, `자동마감` means withdrawing the EARLY
intervention and returning to the original automatic-close timeline.  It is not
a new close method.

Admission requires:

- SCHEDULED operation;
- the original AUTO trigger time has not arrived;
- the EARLY close is not complete; and
- the fill restrictions below are satisfied.

EARLY + ROUTINE may return while incomplete.  EARLY + MARKET,
CURRENT_PRICE, or PROFIT_LOSS may return only when no SELL quantity has filled.
An unfilled SELL is cancelled and confirmed before return.  Any partial or full
SELL fill blocks return.

No past AUTO Event is created retroactively.  CONTINUOUS/manual/manual+ATS has
no AUTO timeline and therefore no return-to-AUTO action.

==================================================
15. CONTINUOUS And SCHEDULED Liquidation Policy
==================================================

Ordinary CONTINUOUS/manual/manual+ATS operation with no legitimate close or
liquidation route displays `-`, performs no liquidation, and never creates AUTO.

When an operator enters EARLY, the environment's snapshotted default
liquidation setting becomes the current Operation's post-close liquidation
policy.  Activating that policy does not execute it immediately.  MARKET or
CURRENT_PRICE execution begins only at the applicable liquidation start
boundary; CARRYOVER performs no action there and waits for section 7 cleanup.

When SCHEDULED enters AUTO, its active Operation snapshot likewise fixes the
default post-close liquidation policy.  Close policy progresses until
completion or the liquidation start boundary.  Holding zero completes;
positive holding at the boundary enters section 4 unless the proven close-stage
route is CARRYOVER.

==================================================
16. Global Default And Per-Stock One-Operation Override
==================================================

Environment settings are persistent global defaults.  Stock context-menu Early
Close and individual liquidation choices are volatile overrides for that stock
and current Operation only.  They do not mutate another stock or the global
default.

At Operation completion, a per-stock override expires.  A later Operation again
starts from its then-current global default unless a new authorized override is
created.

==================================================
17. Individual Liquidation
==================================================

Individual liquidation is setting-only.  It stores one per-stock,
one-Operation override such as N minutes/MARKET, N minutes/CURRENT_PRICE, or
CARRYOVER.

Setting or replacing it must not cancel an order, create a Candidate or Queue
record, call SendOrder, enter close, or enter liquidation.  At its calculated
time boundary, MARKET or CURRENT_PRICE for that stock enters section 4.
CARRYOVER performs no action at that boundary and waits for the section 7
common pending cleanup.  Other stocks retain the global boundary.

The setting is replaceable only before its boundary.  It is frozen on entry and
cannot be changed afterward.  This contract adds no separate cancel, reset, or
default-restore action; an operator who wants the global values must explicitly
select equivalent values before the boundary.

An individual override expires at Operation completion.

If an individual liquidation boundary arrives during that stock's EARLY close,
the liquidation boundary wins and the handoff sequence in section 4 applies.

==================================================
18. UI Availability And Evidence
==================================================

A policy block should normally be decided by backend authority after click and
reported through the existing Toast boundary.  A QAction may be disabled when
the structural target itself does not exist.  This does not authorize UI-only
admission or a new notification framework.

Internal reason and detailed state remain Event/diagnostic evidence.  User UI
must not replace backend admission or expose an internal reason token when the

==================================================
19. Production / Mock Independence
==================================================

Production and Mock implement the same functional meaning in their own domains.
Production uses Production Runtime, broker Order/Fill/Position, Queue,
SendOrder, and Production writers.  Mock uses its Session, virtual
Order/Fill/Position/PnL, Mock lifecycle, and Mock writers.

Neither domain may read, mutate, or treat the other's authority as its own.
Domain-neutral pure helpers may be reused only when they own no mutable domain
state or side effect.

==================================================
21. Level-Based Admission And User Response
==================================================

Stock context-menu Early Close and individual liquidation are one-Operation
interventions for the selected stock.  They never rewrite the global operation
policy.  Their override expires at Operation completion, and a later Operation
starts again from its then-current global default unless the operator creates a
new authorized override.

For Production user commands, the common user-facing admission precedence is:

1. broker login/basic execution qualification;
2. positive current holding quantity;
3. current Operation participation;
4. feature-specific time admission;
5. close-transition or liquidation-setting mutation rules; and
6. Recovery, integrity, emergency, and other safety rules.

Once a higher level blocks the command, a lower-level user message is not
shown.  The normalized messages are:

- broker qualification: `키움 서버에 로그인되어 있지 않습니다.`;
- no holding: `보유수량이 없습니다.`;
- not a current participant: `현재 운영 대상 종목이 아닙니다.`;
- Early Close time admission: `현재 조기마감 가능한 시간이 아닙니다.`;
- liquidation-setting lock: `청산설정변경이 불가능합니다.`;
- disallowed close-method transition: `현재 마감방식으로 변경할 수 없습니다.`;
- other safety block: `현재 상태에서는 실행할 수 없습니다.`.

The simplified user message does not erase the internal reason.  Existing
Event evidence retains timestamp, stock and Operation identity, requested
action and method, admission level, reason code, active operation mode,
close/liquidation state, relevant time boundary, source, and BLOCKED result.
No new Event subsystem is authorized.

Individual liquidation remains setting-only and one-Operation scoped, but it
requires a positive holding in that current Operation.  With zero holding, no
individual setting is stored and no cancellation, Candidate, Queue, SendOrder,
close, or liquidation mutation occurs.  Existing execution-stage and manual
ATS no-holding guards remain unchanged.

A SCHEDULED Operation admits a new Early Close only inside that Operation's
snapshotted start-to-end operation window and before the applicable liquidation
start.  A CONTINUOUS/manual or manual+ATS Operation admits a new Early Close
only from regular-market start up to, but excluding, the applicable liquidation
start.  Pre-market and post-market ATS sessions are neither AUTO trigger axes
nor extensions of the Early Close admission window.  In the absence of an
explicit ATS liquidation intervention, ATS-session passage does not create
AUTO or EARLY close and retains the existing carryover/long-term classification
meaning.

AUTO and operator EARLY entry both require a positive holding.  A zero-holding
AUTO trigger creates no close lifecycle or close order.  A zero-holding EARLY
request is blocked with the no-holding message and creates no close state,
order, or cancellation.  At a liquidation boundary, zero reconciled holding
does not enter a liquidation lifecycle or create a liquidation SELL; only an
otherwise-required pending-order safety cleanup may continue.

Close-method changes during an active close continue to follow section 12.
Liquidation-policy replacement remains permitted before its applicable
boundary and is prohibited after boundary entry under section 6.  Production
and Mock apply these functional meanings through their independent state,
writers, evidence, and execution authorities.  Mock never uses Production
broker-login admission.

==================================================
22. Individual Liquidation Reservation And UI Source Contract
==================================================

This section supersedes the section 21 sentence that requires positive holding
when an individual-liquidation setting is created.  Individual liquidation is
not an immediate close command.  It may be configured before an Operation and
with zero current holding.  Early Close remains a current-Operation
intervention and retains its separate pre-Operation and zero-holding guards.

A pre-Operation individual-liquidation choice is a one-shot
`NEXT_OPERATION` reservation.  At Operation start it is bound to
`CURRENT_OPERATION` and to that exact Operation identity.  Reaching the
effective liquidation time decides execution: positive reconciled holding may
enter the applicable MARKET or CURRENT_PRICE liquidation route; zero holding
creates no new SELL and does not start a liquidation lifecycle merely because
the setting exists.  CARRYOVER is no-action at `liquidation_start` regardless
of holding and proceeds only to the section 7 common cleanup and HOLD meaning.

The environment liquidation policy is the persistent global default.  A
context-menu individual-liquidation choice is a one-shot override.  Within one
program process, the current-value priority is:

1. the active `CURRENT_OPERATION` override for the matching Operation;
2. a `NEXT_OPERATION` reservation owned by the current program session; and
3. the global environment policy.

The existing `PROGRAM_SESSION_ID` is the process identity; no new persistent
source of truth is introduced.  On restart, an unused `NEXT_OPERATION`
reservation from a prior process expires and the global default is used.
Startup cleanup and the Operation-start binding guard both enforce this rule.
A `CURRENT_OPERATION` override is retained for restart recovery only when its
Operation identity matches the active Operation.  A stale current reservation
or identity mismatch is not used and fails closed to the applicable global
fallback.

User display is `N분/시장가` for `MARKET`, `N분/현재가` for
`CURRENT_PRICE`, and exactly `이월` for `CARRYOVER`.  Carryover has no minute
attribute; `N분/이월` is invalid.

For the Mock individual-liquidation context menu, current selection is resolved
from an active `RUNNING`/`CLOSING` Operation snapshot, then a current-process
pending reservation, then `operation_policy.json` global default.  A
`VALIDATION_STOPPED`, `ENDED`, `COMPLETED`, or other historical Operation
snapshot is never a current menu source.  Historical evidence remains intact
and is not deleted or rewritten.

Production and Mock present equivalent user meaning while remaining independent
domains.  Production owns Production state, broker execution, SendOrder,
Chejan, and real Position.  Mock owns Mock Session/lifecycle and virtual
Order/Fill/Position.  Neither domain uses the other's writer, runtime state, or
execution authority.

The existing persistent context menu supports multiple non-terminal changes
from one right-click.  Time or method selection refreshes the same menu instance
without closing it.  Outside click, Escape, context invalidation, and existing
terminal actions close it.  Selecting `CARRYOVER` disables the time submenu;
returning to `MARKET` or `CURRENT_PRICE` enables it again.
