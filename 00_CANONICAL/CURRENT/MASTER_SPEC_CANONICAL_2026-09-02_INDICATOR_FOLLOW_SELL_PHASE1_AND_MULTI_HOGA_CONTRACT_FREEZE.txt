2. SELL Preset Selection Contract
==================================================

SELL settings A, B, and C are three stored execution presets. They are not
three parts of one composite execution plan.

- Exactly one preset is selected at a time.
- Selecting A clears B and C; selecting B clears A and C; selecting C clears A
  and B.
- The final selected preset cannot be unchecked to leave zero selections.
- The two unselected presets retain their saved settings for fast switching.
- One SELL signal uses only Perform 1 of the one selected preset.
- A persisted or effective state with zero or multiple selected presets is
  invalid and fails closed.
- Invalid persisted/effective selection is not silently repaired by choosing a
  first or default preset.

The existing checkbox visual form may remain, but its behavior is mutually
exclusive. The Routine-owned Mapper and Commit Validator preserve the
exactly-one invariant through Effective Rules.

4. SELL MULTI_HOGA Confirmed Future Contract
==================================================

SELL MULTI_HOGA is not complete in Production. Its confirmed implementation
contract is nevertheless fixed as follows.

Identity and hierarchy:
- One SELL Signal creates one Execution Process with N Child Orders.
- Every child retains the same `source_signal_id` and
  `execution_process_id`.
- Every child has a unique `execution_id` and `order_id`.
- `source_signal_id` must never be changed, suffixed, or otherwise fabricated
  to evade duplicate detection.

Quantity and order sequence:
- Planned quantity is split as evenly as possible across child orders.
- Remainder shares are assigned first to the basis order price, then in order
  of hoga distance from that basis price.
- Actual child submission order is basis order price, then nearer hoga, then
  farther hoga.
- If current sellable remainder is smaller than the planned child count, it is
  not forcibly fragmented. All remaining quantity becomes one basis-price
  LIMIT SELL.

Child failure and automatic-trading continuity:
- Failure of one child does not stop attempts for the other planned children.
- After the first complete batch attempt, the latest Broker, Queue, Chejan,
  Position, and identity evidence is refreshed.
- Missing planned attempts are replanned from that latest state.
- No new fixed-N retry policy is introduced.
- Normal quantity reduction and partial Fill are recalculated and may continue.
- A clearly transient SendOrder/API failure may be replenished after current
  state is known.
- If order existence, Queue/Chejan/Position state, or identity consistency is
  uncertain, resending the same order is forbidden because it could duplicate
  an order. Existing Review/reconciliation isolation is used instead.
- One error must not unnecessarily stop unrelated automatic-trading continuity.
