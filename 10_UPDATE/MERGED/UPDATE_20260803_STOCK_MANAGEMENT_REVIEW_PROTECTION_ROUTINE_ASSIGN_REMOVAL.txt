9. Review Management Resolution Actions

Review Management exposes three distinct operator actions. They must not be
treated as equivalent delete or release operations.

- `복귀` preserves the stock's project data and restores or retains its existing
  routine relationship after the review cause is resolved and the integrity
  gate passes.
- `미지정` preserves the stock's project data but removes its routine
  relationship after the review cause is resolved and the integrity gate
  passes. The stock remains registered in Stock Management as unassigned.
- `강제초기화` is the manual final-disposal path for a Review Management stock
  that cannot be normalized through `복귀` or `미지정`. It permanently removes
  the central `stocks/<stock>` project data, registration, routine relationship,
  and Review Management entry. The result is an unregistered stock and is not
  recoverable from the project.

The normal Stock Management `종목초기화` path and Review Management
`강제초기화` may have the same final local-data removal result, but their entry
policy differs. Normal reset remains subject to its existing protected-state
eligibility and blocks Review Management stocks. Forced reset is available only
as an explicit operator action for a Review Management target and is not blocked
merely because the target is in Review Management.

Forced reset discards local project data only. It does not clear real broker
holdings, cancel unresolved broker orders, change broker order state, or
normalize the actual account. Available holding or unresolved-order information
may be shown as a warning, but those broker realities are not processed by the
forced reset itself.

The system must not introduce or infer an automatic `UNRECOVERABLE` or disposal
state. Forced reset remains an explicit operator decision. Target identity and
path safety must be verified, deletion must not reach another stock or a project
root, and failed deletion or failed post-delete verification must not be
reported as success.
