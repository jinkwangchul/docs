5. Completed Event Journal Phase 1

- `OPERATOR_SYSTEM_DECISION`
- `OPERATOR_OPERATION_DECISION`
- `OPERATOR_SETTING_DECISION`
- `OPERATOR_ORDER_DECISION`

Operator choice and Production outcome are separate evidence. `ACCEPTED` does
not mean backend/order/broker/fill success.

P2 setting/management:

- `SETTING_CHANGED`
- `TRADING_TIME_CHANGED`
- `ATS_CHANGED`
- `ROUTINE_CHANGED`
- `ROUTINE_INSTANCE_CREATED`
- `ROUTINE_INSTANCE_DELETED`

Only successful canonical mutation plus required read-back and a real
before/after difference produces a change event. Same-value, Cancel, unsaved,
validation-failed, writer-failed, and read-back-failed paths produce no success
event.

P3 operational warnings/errors:

- `PROCESSING_ERROR`
- `RUNTIME_WARNING`
- `INTEGRITY_WARNING`

Important operation, emergency, budget/buffer evidence, Runtime, and integrity
failures are recorded without duplicating existing Recovery/account/connection/
order evidence. Unchanged abnormal polling does not repeat events; recovery and
recurrence may create a new event.

Corrected existing boundaries:

- `PNL_CYCLE_BOUNDARY_CREATED` records only creation of a confirmable PnL cycle
  boundary.
- `LIQUIDATION_REQUESTED` is recorded after atomic state write, verified
  read-back, and `STOCK_APPLIED`; operator decision remains separate.
