2. Main Monitoring Badge Set

- The left badge order is:
  `유효 / 그룹 / 루틴 / 종목 / 보유 / 가격 / 수익 / 미결 / 한도`.
- Legacy `전체` and `운용` vertical tabs are not displayed.
- A `과거` badge is not part of this monitoring contract.
- `유효` is an independent toggle.
- `그룹`, `루틴`, and `종목` are mutually exclusive hierarchy selections.
- `보유`, `가격`, `수익`, `미결`, and `한도` are monitoring metric controls.
- The user-facing profit term in this monitoring surface is `수익`; internal
  data fields, calculations, storage formats, and APIs are unchanged.

3. Badge Interaction Contract

- `유효 + 종목` displays only valid stock rows as a flat presentation.
- In that combination, parent and instance rows are hidden without creating a
  second data model or changing the underlying hierarchy.
- Selecting `그룹` disables `보유`, `가격`, `수익`, `미결`, and `한도`.
- Selecting `루틴` or `종목` enables the supported metric controls.
- Separators after `유효` and after `종목` are non-interactive visual group
  boundaries. They cannot receive click or focus behavior.
- Selected badges retain the existing green active presentation.
- Enabled but unselected badges use the current dark-gray text presentation.
- Disabled badges retain the existing light-gray presentation.
- Further badge color refinement is not part of this freeze.

4. Budget Concept Boundary

The following concepts are confirmed for later budget design:

- `전체예산 설정`
- `종목 최초예산`
- `예산방식`: `금액` or `주수`
- `현재 구매예산`: the purchase budget determined during additional buying
  or active buying by the applicable amount basis or round basis
- `운영금액`, `완충금액`, and `한도금액` within total-budget operation

These terms establish a vocabulary boundary only. This closure does not define
or implement:

- an unconfirmed calculation formula
- hierarchical budget allocation
- `사용예산` or `잔여예산`
- new persistence fields, Runtime state, Queue behavior, or Broker behavior
