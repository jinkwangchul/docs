1. Policy Option Boundary

Close Policy (`마감정책`) consists of:
- `루틴마감`
- `이월`
- `시장가`
- `현재가`
- `손/익절`

Liquidation Policy (`청산정책`) consists of:
- `이월`
- `시장가`
- `현재가`

`현재가` and `시장가` are order methods.

`손/익절` is a Close Policy behavior. It is not a Liquidation Policy option.

The project does not have a separate limit-price (`지정가`) feature.
`현재가` means that the order uses the current price observed when the
applicable function is activated.

2. Pending-order Cancellation Boundary

The following transitions require the applicable pending orders to be
cancelled under the existing order, Queue, SendOrder, and Chejan evidence
contracts:

Close Policy:
- `시장가`
- `현재가`
- `손/익절`

Liquidation Policy:
- `시장가`
- `현재가`

Exceptions:
- `루틴마감`
- `이월`

This section confirms only which policy choices require pending-order
cancellation. It does not define a new cancellation implementation, Queue
format, Writer, completion shortcut, or Source of Truth.

3. Close Policy Transition Matrix

From `루틴마감`:
- transition to `이월`: allowed
- transition to `시장가`: allowed
- transition to `현재가`: allowed
- transition to `손/익절`: allowed

From `이월`:
- transition to `시장가`: allowed
- transition to `현재가`: allowed
- transition to `손/익절`: allowed
- transition to `루틴마감`: conditionally allowed

The transition from `이월` to `루틴마감` is allowed only when all of the
following are true:
- no close behavior defined by the Routine has started
- no actual order has been created
- no actual buy has occurred
- no actual sell has occurred

If any one of these conditions is not satisfied, transition from `이월` to
`루틴마감` is forbidden.

From `현재가`:
- transition to `시장가`: allowed
- transition to `손/익절`: allowed
- transition to `루틴마감`: forbidden
- transition to `이월`: forbidden

From `손/익절`:
- transition to `현재가`: allowed
- transition to `시장가`: allowed
- transition to `루틴마감`: forbidden
- transition to `이월`: forbidden

From `시장가`:
- transition to `현재가`: forbidden
- transition to `손/익절`: forbidden
- transition to `루틴마감`: forbidden
- transition to `이월`: forbidden

Therefore:
- `현재가` and `손/익절` allow transition in both directions.
- `현재가` may transition to `시장가`; `시장가` may not transition back to
  `현재가`.
- `손/익절` may transition to `시장가`; `시장가` may not transition back to
  `손/익절`.

4. Execution-progress Principle

Transition eligibility is determined by actual execution progress, not only by
the policy option name.

After pending-order cancellation has started, the operation must not move
backward to `루틴마감` or `이월`, because those behaviors depend on the
pre-cancellation execution state.

This contract does not authorize cancellation completion to be inferred from a
SendOrder return value. Existing Chejan and broker-evidence boundaries remain
authoritative.

5. Automatic Close And Early Close

The same Transition Contract applies when an Automatic Close (`자동마감`)
operation is changed to an Early Close (`조기마감`) operation.

Automatic Close and Early Close remain distinct entry purposes under the
existing contracts. Applying the same transition rules does not merge their
names, persistent settings, command identity, or invocation behavior.
